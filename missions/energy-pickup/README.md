# Energy Pickup

A four-step intermediate mission with three colored zones and one pushable
object. The robot leaves home, drives to the depot, pushes the cyan energy
block off the depot, and deposits it in the green storage zone.

## Steps

1. **Leave the blue home zone**            (5 pts)
2. **Reach the orange depot**              (15 pts, requires #1)
3. **Push the energy block off the depot** (15 pts, requires #2)
4. **Get the energy block to storage**     (25 pts, requires #3)

Total: **60 points**.

## Field

- **Blue HOME** at the bottom-left
- **Orange DEPOT** at the top-middle (centred near y = 925)
- **Green STORAGE** at the bottom-right
- One **cyan energy block** (the obstacle) starts on the depot.
